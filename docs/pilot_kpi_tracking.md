# Pilot KPI Tracking

## Objective
Track technical reliability and business impact weekly for each pilot site.

## Weekly Reliability KPIs
- Scheduler job success rate (%):
  - Target: `>=95%`
- P1 incidents count:
  - Target: `0`
- Weekly ROI report generated:
  - Target: `Yes`

## Weekly Business KPIs
- Labor % (avg)
- Revenue per labor hour (avg)
- Weekly revenue
- Weekly net profit
- Week-over-week net profit delta

## Review Cadence
- Weekly owner/manager review:
  - Date/time: `________________`
  - Attendees: `________________`
- Agenda:
  - KPI movement
  - Data quality issues
  - Action decisions for next week

## Escalation Rules
- Trigger immediate review if:
  - Job success falls below `95%`
  - Net profit drops materially for `2` consecutive weeks
  - Labor % increases materially for `2` consecutive weeks
  - ROI report cannot be generated due to data gaps
