# Clubhouse Autopilot Pilot Offer

## Purpose
Run a 30-day paid pilot to validate measurable business impact before broader rollout.

## Target Customer
- Independent specialty cafes and small multi-site groups using Square + Deputy.

## Pilot Scope (Included)
- Daily ingest, prediction, and tomorrow plan workflow.
- Shift nudges and operational recommendations.
- Profitability pipeline with daily P&L metrics.
- Xero sync for supplier-cost-informed COGS (if connected).
- Weekly ROI summary (dashboard + API/script output).

## Pilot Deliverables
- Initial setup and data integration.
- Baseline KPI capture (week 0).
- Weekly ROI review with owner/manager.
- End-of-pilot outcome report and recommendation.

## Commercial Terms (Template)
- Setup fee: `$________`
- Monthly pilot fee: `$________`
- Pilot duration: `30 days`
- Included locations: `1` (extra location fee: `$________`)

## Success Criteria (Pilot Pass)
- Scheduler job success rate >= `95%`.
- At least `1` weekly ROI report generated per site per week.
- No P1 incidents for `2` consecutive weeks.
- Measurable directional improvement in at least 2 of:
  - Labor %
  - Revenue per labor hour
  - Weekly net profit

## Exclusions (Pilot)
- Full enterprise auth/RBAC deployment.
- Subscription billing automation.
- Custom BI/export development beyond standard outputs.

## Post-Pilot Conversion Path
- Convert to monthly subscription.
- Expand to additional sites.
- Add premium support and advisory package.
